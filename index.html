04THE EDITOR

Eight years in the
dark of the edit suite.

MrinmoyKolkata · Worldwide
Mrinmoy EST. 2018

Quiet rooms,
loud decisions.

I started cutting on a borrowed laptop in a kitchen in Kolkata. Music videos for friends, then commercials, then films that took me to rooms I didn't know existed. Somewhere along the way, the timeline stopped feeling like a tool and started feeling like a language.

I work best with directors who treat the edit as a second draft of the script — collaborators willing to murder their darlings for the sake of rhythm. I'm equally at home in a 90-second luxury spot and a 22-minute documentary. The medium changes; the discipline doesn't.
